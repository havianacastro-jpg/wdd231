Mexico City Urban Cycling Club

Individual Project Site Plan

Site Name

Selected Site Name: Mexico City Urban Cycling Club

Reason for selection: This name clearly identifies a community-focused organization dedicated to promoting safe urban cycling, bicycle commuting, and group rides specifically tailored to the environment of Mexico City.

Optional domain availability: mexico-urban-cycling.org

Site Purpose

The website provides an online hub for urban cyclists in Mexico City by offering safe route guides, bicycle maintenance tips, urban road safety resources, community event schedules, and an interactive membership subscription form.

Scenarios

Target audience questions that will drive the content of the website:

  • Where can I find safe cycling lanes and recommended urban routes for commuting in Mexico City?
  • How can I register my membership or sign up for upcoming weekend group rides?
  • What essential gear and waterproof accessories are recommended for cycling during the rainy season?
  • How do I contact the club directors or find local bicycle repair workshops?

Color Scheme

The color palette is designed to reflect professionalism, visibility, and modern urban mobility:

Primary Color (#1A365D)
Used for Headers, Main Titles, and Footer
Accent Color (#D69E2E)
Used for Highlights, Borders, and Call-to-Actions

Typography

The typography balances clean modern headers with high legibility for body paragraphs:

  • Headings: Montserrat (Sans-serif, bold, professional styling for all h1, h2, h3 elements).
  • Body Text: Open Sans (Sans-serif, clean readability for paragraphs, lists, and general content).

Wireframe

Below is the structural layout description for mobile and desktop viewports of the Home page.

Mobile View (Small Screen)

  • Header with logo and hamburger navigation menu.
  • Hero section with a welcome banner and primary call-to-action button.
  • Stacked single-column layout containing featured content cards and upcoming events.
  • Footer containing copyright, social media links, and last modified date.

Desktop View (Large Screen)

  • Header with horizontal navigation links aligned to the right.
  • Hero section with background imagery and prominent headline.
  • Multi-column grid layout (2 to 3 columns) displaying club benefits, latest news, and quick navigation.
  • Standard footer layout.
